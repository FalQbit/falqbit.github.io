The rules

Code decides. Nobody else can.

No clawback

Nobody can take tokens out of your account. Not the contract, not the creator.

No admin backdoor

The freeze role belongs to a public smart contract, not to a person or a private key.

Keyless accounts allowed

Liquidity pools and contracts have no private key to attack, so they can hold FALQBIT too.

Go back to classic, get locked again

If an account rekeys away from Falcon, anyone can ask the contract to lock it. Your tokens stay yours and unlock again when you return to Falcon.
