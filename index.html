The rules

Code decides. Nobody else can.

No clawback

Nobody can take tokens out of your account. Not the contract, not the creator.

No admin backdoor

The freeze role belongs to a public smart contract, not to a person or a private key.

Approved code only

Pools and contracts can hold FALQBIT only if the code that controls them is on the gate's approved list, Tinyman v2 is the only app on the list. The approver role has been renounced, so the list can never change.

Nothing can be changed

The token has no manager and the gate contract cannot be updated or deleted. The rules you read here are the rules forever.

Go back to classic, get locked again

If an account rekeys away from Falcon, anyone can ask the contract to lock it. Your tokens stay yours and unlock again when you return to Falcon.

Honest limits

What FALQBIT does not solve.

  • Algorand consensus is still classical. Accounts and chain history are post-quantum; block voting is on Algorand's roadmap for 2027.
  • Relocking happens after the fact. If an unlocked account rekeys back to a classic key, it stays unlocked until someone calls relock. Anyone can, and they earn the account's deposit for it.
  • Only Tinyman v2 pools. The approved list is final, so other exchanges and new contracts cannot hold FALQBIT. Airdrops and rewards go straight to unlocked quantum accounts.
  • Code-controlled addresses. Application and pool addresses are hashes. Whether such an address could be attacked by a future quantum computer depends on Algorand's ledger roadmap, not on this contract.
  • Tinyman protocol fees. Fees paid in FALQBIT cannot be claimed by Tinyman's classic fee account. This does not affect traders.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

How do I get a quantum account?

In Pera Wallet, tap + to add an account and choose Add Quantum Account. You can also rekey an existing account to a Falcon-1024 key and keep the same address.

Can I lose my FALQBIT?

Nobody can take them: there is no clawback. If you send to a classic account, the transfer is refused and the tokens stay with you. As with any crypto, losing your recovery phrase means losing access.

What happens if I switch back to a classic key?

Anyone can relock your holding. Your tokens stay yours, and you can unlock again after switching back to a Falcon key.

Why are the fees higher?

A Falcon-1024 signature is much larger than a classic one, so Algorand charges about three minimum fees for it: around 0.003 ALGO per transaction.

Why can't I buy it on an exchange?

An exchange would have to hold FALQBIT in quantum accounts. Any exchange can do that and unlock its accounts the same way you do.
