HOW IT WORKS

Share your link

Every unlocked holder with 1M+ FALQBIT held 7+ days gets a personal invite link. Your friend opens it, unlocks with Falcon-1024 and buys FALQBIT — the referral is recorded on-chain, inside the unlock itself. No sign-ups, no cookies, no trust needed.

Make sure your friend opens your link before unlocking — the invite is written into the unlock transaction and can't be added afterwards.
