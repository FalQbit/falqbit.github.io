01The problem

Almost every crypto wallet today is protected by elliptic-curve signatures such as Ed25519 or ECDSA. They are secure against today's computers. A large enough quantum computer running Shor's algorithm could derive a private key from a public key, and then sign transactions as the owner.

Nobody knows when such a machine will exist. But public keys stay on-chain forever, and standards bodies are already moving: NIST selected Falcon as a post-quantum signature standard, and governments have set migration deadlines for critical systems around 2030. Assets that cannot move to post-quantum keys will be the easiest to attack.

Algorand added Falcon-1024 accounts to its MainNet, and wallets such as Pera now let anyone create a Quantum Account. What was missing was a reason to use one.

02The solution

FALQBIT is the first token that only post-quantum accounts can hold. A classic account cannot receive it, hold it or trade it: the network itself refuses the transfer.

  • For users, it is a simple, practical reason to move to a Quantum Account, with rewards for doing it.
  • For Algorand, it is a live, public showcase of post-quantum accounts being used every day.
  • For everyone, the rules are code: no admin, no clawback, no way to change them later.

03How it works

Frozen by default

FALQBIT was created with default frozen set. Every account that adds the token starts locked. The freeze role does not belong to a person: it belongs to the gate contract (application 3760919863). The token has no manager and no clawback, so these settings can never change.

Proving a Falcon key

An account is unlocked only if it proves that a Falcon-1024 key signs for it. Algorand derives a Quantum Account's address from its Falcon public key:

address = SHA512-256("PQA" ‖ "f1" ‖ salt ‖ public_key)

The user sends the public key and salt to the gate. The contract recomputes the address, checks that it matches the account (or the account's current signer, if it was rekeyed), and then unfreezes the holding. The unlock page at falqbit.com/unlock does all of this in one approval in Pera Wallet. A small deposit (about 0.03 ALGO) pays for the record the contract keeps.

Going back to classic gets you locked again

If an unlocked account later rekeys to a classic key, anyone can call the contract's relock method. The holding is frozen again and the caller receives the account's deposit as a reward. The tokens stay with their owner and unlock again once the account returns to a Falcon key.

Pools and contracts

Pools and other smart contracts have no signing key. They can hold FALQBIT only if the program that controls them is on the gate's approved list. Tinyman v2 is the only approved program, so any Tinyman v2 pool can hold FALQBIT. The approver role has been renounced: the list can never grow or shrink.

07Joining and trading

  1. Create a Quantum Account in Pera Wallet (Add account → Add Quantum Account), or rekey an existing account to a Falcon-1024 key.
  2. Unlock it at falqbit.com/unlock. Adding the token and unlocking happen in one step.
  3. Buy, sell or hold on falqbit.com/trade, in the FALQBIT app or on Tinyman. All three use the same Tinyman pool. Price and charts are on Vestige.
  4. Pay and get paid with QR codes in the app. Every payment is approved in Pera; the app never holds keys.

Fees. A Falcon-1024 signature is larger than a classic one, so each transaction costs about three minimum fees, around 0.003 ALGO. Tinyman charges its usual 0.3% swap fee, which goes to liquidity providers.

From FALQBIT to local currency. Today: swap FALQBIT to ALGO on Tinyman, then ALGO to USDC. On falqbit.com/trade this takes one approval. Then send the USDC (or ALGO) to a regulated exchange that supports Algorand, sell it for your local currency (EUR, USD and others) and withdraw to your bank. The project itself never converts FALQBIT to fiat money.

Exchanges. Pools on DEXs other than Tinyman v2 cannot hold FALQBIT. Centralized exchanges can list it by holding it in quantum accounts and unlocking them like any user.

08Security and limits

What the code guarantees

  • No clawback. Nobody can take tokens out of an account, not even the creator.
  • No admin key. The freeze role is a public contract.
  • Immutable. The gate cannot be updated or deleted, the token has no manager, and the approver role is renounced.
  • Open source. The gate is under 200 lines of Algorand Python, published with its compiled program and ABI. An independent automated review found no way for a classic account to unlock. There has been no professional audit yet.

What FALQBIT does not solve

  • Consensus. Algorand's accounts can be post-quantum, but block voting still uses classical keys. Post-quantum consensus is on Algorand's own roadmap.
  • Relocking is after the fact. An account that rekeys back to classic stays unlocked until someone calls relock.
  • Code-controlled addresses. Pool and application addresses are hashes, not keys. Their long-term quantum safety depends on Algorand's protocol, not on this contract.
  • One DEX family. The approved list is final: only Tinyman v2 pools can hold FALQBIT.

10Risks and legal notice

FALQBIT is not an investment product. It gives no right to profits, dividends, interest, governance or redemption, and nothing in this document is financial advice or a promise of returns.

  • Price risk. The price is set only by the market and can fall to zero. Liquidity is small and prices can move sharply.
  • Smart contract risk. The gate is immutable and has not been professionally audited. A bug could not be fixed.
  • Reward risk. Rewards depend on the project continuing to send them. They can shrink when many people hold.
  • Key risk. Losing a recovery phrase means losing the tokens. Nobody can recover them.
  • Technology risk. Post-quantum cryptography and Algorand's Falcon accounts are new. Wallet or protocol changes could affect how FALQBIT works.
  • Regulatory risk. Crypto-asset rules, including the EU's MiCA regulation, keep changing and could affect how FALQBIT can be offered, listed or used in some countries.

Only acquire FALQBIT with money you can afford to lose. Always check the official asset ID 3760920399: anyone can create a token with the same name.
