How hold-to-earn works

Minimum 1,000,000 FALQBIT

Your wallet needs at least 1M FALQBIT on payout day to receive any reward.

0.5% monthly (1% in Nov 2026)

Each payout is 0.5% of your lowest balance since the 1st. The launch month (November 2026) pays 1%.

Cap: 500,000 per wallet

No single wallet gets more than 500K FALQBIT per month, regardless of balance.

Monthly pool: 15,000,000

The total paid out each month is capped at 15M FALQBIT, shared proportionally among all holders.

Paid on the 25th

Rewards are paid on the 25th of every month, straight to your account from the public Rewards wallet.

No staking needed

Tokens stay in your own quantum account. You can trade or send at any time — the payout uses your lowest balance since the 1st.
